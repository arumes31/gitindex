What data is processed

This is a static, content-focused website. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising or third-party analytics. No external scripts, fonts or stylesheets are loaded — all assets are served from this domain, so your browser does not contact any third party while you read.

Server log files

When you access this site, the hosting infrastructure may automatically process technical data (IP address, date and time, requested URL, referrer, user agent). This processing is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR) in operating and securing the website. Log data is not merged with other data sources.

Mirrored images

Images contained in README files (for example badges or screenshots) are fetched by our server and re-served from this domain through an image proxy. Your browser therefore does not connect to GitHub or other image hosts when viewing a project page.

External links

Pages contain links to GitHub and project homepages. When you follow such a link, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies. We have no influence over their data processing.
